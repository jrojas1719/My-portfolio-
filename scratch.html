The Deal Pipeline

Every deal I've ever closed moved through the same five stages. Here's what actually happens at each one.

  1. Prospecting

    Finding the right people before trying to talk to anyone.

    • Research the account before the first touch
    • Warm intros beat cold calls almost every time
    • Disqualify fast — bad-fit leads waste everyone's time

  2. Qualifying

    Figuring out if there's a real problem worth solving.

    • Ask about budget honestly, early
    • Find the actual decision maker
    • Listen for urgency — no urgency, no deal

  3. Pitching

    Turning a product into a specific answer to their problem.

    • Lead with their pain point, not your features
    • Use one story, not ten statistics
    • Leave room for them to talk more than you do

  4. Closing

    Making it easy to say yes.

    • Address the real objection, not the polite one
    • Give a clear next step, every time
    • Silence after the ask is your best friend

  5. Retention

    The sale that matters most is the one that renews.

    • Check in before they need you to
    • Turn happy clients into referrals
    • Treat renewal like a first pitch, not a formality
